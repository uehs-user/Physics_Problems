# Pendulum Physics — Formulas & Objects
 
## Variables
 
| Symbol | Object | Unit |
|--------|--------|------|
| `T` | Period — time for one full oscillation | seconds (s) |
| `L` | Length — from pivot to centre of bob | metres (m) |
| `g` | Gravitational acceleration | m/s² |
| `f` | Frequency — oscillations per second | hertz (Hz) |
 
---
 
## Formulas
 
### 1. Period of a simple pendulum
$$T = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{L}{g}}$$
 
### 2. Period ratio between two gravitational fields
$$\frac{T_{moon}}{T_{earth}} = \sqrt{\frac{g_{earth}}{g_{moon}}}$$
 
### 3. Moon period from Earth period
$$T_{moon} = T_{earth} \times \sqrt{\frac{g_{earth}}{g_{moon}}} = 4 \times \sqrt{6} \approx 9.8 \text{ s}$$
 
### 4. Required length for a given period
$$L = g \left(\frac{T}{2\pi}\right)^2$$
 
### 5. Length for T = 1 s on Earth
$$L = 9.81 \times \left(\frac{1}{2\pi}\right)^2 \approx 0.248 \text{ m}$$
