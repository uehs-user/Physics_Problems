# Wave Equation Analysis

Given the wave equation:
$$y(x, t) = 0.05 \sin(2\pi x - 50\pi t)$$

We compare this to the standard traveling wave form:
$$y(x, t) = A \sin(kx - \omega t)$$

### 1. Identification of Parameters
By looking at the equation, we can extract:
*   **Amplitude ($A$):** $0.05$
*   **Wave number ($k$):** $2\pi$
*   **Angular frequency ($\omega$):** $50\pi$

---

### a) Amplitude ($A$)
The **Amplitude** represents the maximum displacement of the wave.
*   **Result:** $A = 0.05 \text{ m}$

### b) Wavelength ($\lambda$)
The **Wavelength** is the physical distance between successive crests. It is calculated using the wave number ($k$):
$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{k}$$
$$\lambda = \frac{2\pi}{2\pi}$$
*   **Result:** $\lambda = 1 \text{ m}$

### c) Frequency ($f$)
The **Frequency** is how many cycles occur per second. It is calculated using the angular frequency ($\omega$):
$$f = \frac{\omega}{2\pi}$$
$$f = \frac{50\pi}{2\pi}$$
*   **Result:** $f = 25 \text{ Hz}$

### d) Wave Speed ($v$)
The **Wave Speed** is the velocity at which the wave propagates through the medium. It can be found by multiplying frequency and wavelength:
$$v = f \cdot \lambda$$
$$v = 25 \text{ Hz} \cdot 1 \text{ m}$$
*   **Result:** $v = 25 \text{ m/s}$
